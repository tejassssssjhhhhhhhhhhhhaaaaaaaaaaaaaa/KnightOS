# Walkthrough — Wave 3: Connected Intelligence Layer

KnightOS is now a fully connected operating system, capable of ingesting knowledge from the cloud and securing your data with encrypted backups.

## Key Features

### 1. Gmail Knowledge Engine
KnightOS now scans your Gmail metadata to extract structured financial transactions, travel itineraries, and career milestones.
- **Privacy First**: No email bodies are stored.
- **Automation**: Receipts automatically update your Finance Tracker.

### 2. Encrypted Cloud Backup
Full system state (Database + Documents) is now protected by AES-256 encryption and backed up to your private Google Drive.
- **Self-Healing**: Restore your entire life-OS on a new device with a single click.

### 3. Unified Health & Schedule
- **Health Connect**: Steps, heart rate, and sleep data are synced from your wearable ecosystem.
- **Calendar Intelligence**: Your schedule is now woven into your Missions and Goals, providing a holistic view of your time.

### 4. Sync Center
A new centralized hub to monitor all connected intelligence sources.
- View real-time sync status.
- Trigger manual backups.
- Track duplicate prevention statistics.
