# KnightOS Engineering Decisions

## Decision Log

---

### Decision 001

Date: 2026-08-03

Title:
Adopt Four-Team Development Model

Decision:
KnightOS development will be managed by four permanent AI teams:
- Platform Team
- Product Team
- Intelligence Team
- Mission Control

Reason:
Improve scalability, ownership, and parallel development.

Status:
Approved

---

### Decision 002

Date: 2026-08-03

Title:
Milestone-Based Development

Decision:
All Version 5 work shall progress milestone by milestone.

No team may advance until Mission Control approves the current milestone.

Status:
Approved

---

### Decision 003

Date: 2026-08-03

Title:
Mission Control Authority

Decision:
Mission Control is responsible for project coordination, validation, documentation, regression management, and milestone approval.

Mission Control does not implement features.

Status:
Approved

---

## Decision Rules

Every decision must include:

- Title
