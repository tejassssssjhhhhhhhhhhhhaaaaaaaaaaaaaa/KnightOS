# KnightOS Product Roadmap

## Vision
To evolve KnightOS from a reactive tool into a proactive personal companion through autonomous reasoning and real-time perception.

## Current Milestone: Sprint B (Intelligence & Refinement)
*Focus: Enhancing local reasoning cycles and expanding data ingestion depth.*

### Short-Term Goals (Next 30 Days)
- **Quantized LLM Integration**: Transition perception reasoning to a local execution path for lower latency.
- **Orb Expressiveness**: Visual feedback improvements for "Thinking" and "Acting" states.
- **Finance Depth**: Automated categorization of transaction metadata.

### Mid-Term Goals (Next 90 Days)
- **Voice Intelligence**: Full local STT/TTS integration.
- **Document OCR**: Knowledge Vault ingestion from physical documents.
- **Cross-Module Triggers**: Events in Finance triggering Planner actions.

### Long-Term Vision (Next 6+ Months)
- **Sensor Fusion**: High-frequency accelerometer and proximity data integration.
- **Edge-AI Separation**: Moving heavy logic to dedicated isolates.
- **WASM Reasoning**: WebAssembly for high-performance math models.
