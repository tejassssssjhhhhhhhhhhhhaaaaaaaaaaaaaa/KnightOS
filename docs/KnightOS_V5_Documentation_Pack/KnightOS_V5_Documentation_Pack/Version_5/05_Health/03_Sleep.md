# Knight OS Version 5 - Sleep Management

# Overview

The Sleep Management module helps users monitor, understand, and improve their sleep quality through intelligent tracking, analysis, personalized recommendations, and healthy bedtime routines. It combines data from connected devices, user inputs, and AI analysis to provide meaningful insights into sleep patterns.

The objective is to encourage healthier sleep habits while respecting user privacy and individual preferences.

---

# Objectives

- Monitor sleep duration
- Analyze sleep quality
- Improve sleep consistency
- Build healthy bedtime routines
- Detect unusual sleep patterns
- Provide AI-powered sleep recommendations

---

# Sleep Tracking

The module should record:

- Bedtime
- Wake-up time
- Total sleep duration
- Sleep efficiency
- Time to fall asleep
- Night awakenings
- Sleep consistency
- Nap duration
- Sleep debt

Where supported, additional metrics such as heart rate and blood oxygen may also be collected.

---

# Sleep Stages

Supported sleep stages may include:

- Awake
- Light Sleep
- Deep Sleep
- REM Sleep

Stage information depends on the connected tracking device.

---

# Daily Sleep Summary

Each day should include:

- Total sleep duration
- Sleep score
- Bedtime
- Wake-up time
- Sleep stage breakdown
- Sleep interruptions
- Overall sleep quality

---

# Sleep Goals

Users should be able to configure:

- Target bedtime
- Target wake-up time
- Daily sleep duration
- Weekly sleep consistency
- Nap limits
- Sleep schedule reminders

Goals should adapt to changing routines.

---

# AI Sleep Coach

The AI may recommend:

- Sleep earlier tonight
- Maintain a consistent bedtime
- Reduce screen time before sleeping
- Avoid caffeine late in the day
- Improve bedroom environment
- Increase sleep duration
- Follow a relaxing bedtime routine

Recommendations should be personalized and non-intrusive.

---

# Bedtime Routine

Knight OS may assist with:

- Bedtime reminders
- Wind-down notifications
- Automatic Do Not Disturb
- Dim screen brightness
- Relaxation exercises
- Meditation shortcuts
- Alarm preparation

These routines should be fully customizable.

Historical reports help identify habits and improvements over time.
