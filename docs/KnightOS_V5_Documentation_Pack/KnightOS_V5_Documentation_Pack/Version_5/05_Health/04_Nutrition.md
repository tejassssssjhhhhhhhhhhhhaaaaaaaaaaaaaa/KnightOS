# Knight OS Version 5 - Nutrition Management

# Overview

The Nutrition Management module helps users maintain healthy eating habits by tracking meals, hydration, calories, nutrients, and dietary goals. It combines user input, AI analysis, and connected health services to provide personalized nutritional guidance and long-term wellness insights.

The module is designed to support healthy lifestyle choices rather than strict dieting, giving users complete flexibility and control.

---

# Objectives

- Track daily food intake
- Monitor nutritional balance
- Encourage healthy eating habits
- Support hydration goals
- Help users achieve nutrition targets
- Deliver AI-powered dietary insights

---

# Meal Tracking

Users should be able to record:

- Breakfast
- Lunch
- Dinner
- Snacks
- Beverages

Each meal may include:

- Meal name
- Time consumed
- Ingredients
- Serving size
- Calories
- Notes
- Photos (optional)

---

# Nutritional Information

The module should monitor:

- Calories
- Protein
- Carbohydrates
- Fat
- Fiber
- Sugar
- Sodium
- Vitamins
- Minerals
- Water intake

Additional nutrients should be supported as nutritional databases evolve.

---

# Hydration Tracking

Users should be able to:

- Record water intake
- Set daily hydration goals
- View hydration progress
- Receive hydration reminders
- Track beverage history

Hydration recommendations may adjust based on activity level and weather (when available).

---

# Nutrition Goals

Users may define goals such as:

- Daily calorie target
- Protein intake
- Carbohydrate intake
- Fat intake
- Water intake
- Weight management
- Healthy eating consistency

Goals should remain editable at any time.

---

# AI Nutrition Coach

The AI may recommend:

- Increase protein intake
- Improve hydration
- Reduce added sugar
- Eat more vegetables
- Maintain balanced meals
- Avoid skipping meals
- Prepare healthier alternatives

Recommendations should be educational, personalized, and non-judgmental.

---

# Food History

Users should be able to review:

- Daily meal logs
- Weekly nutrition summaries
- Monthly eating trends
- Nutrient averages
- Goal achievement history
- Hydration history

Historical data supports long-term habit improvement.

---

# Integration

Nutrition data may integrate with:

- Fitness Tracking
- Sleep Management
- Health Dashboard
- AI Core
- Learning Engine
- Suggestions Engine
- Connected health platforms

Cross-module insights should provide a holistic wellness view.

---

# Privacy

Nutrition information should:

- Require user permission
- Be securely stored
- Support export
- Support deletion
- Never be shared without explicit authorization

---

# Performance Goals

- Fast meal logging
- Efficient nutritional analysis
- Low battery consumption
- Reliable synchronization
- Accurate progress reporting
- Scalable food database support

---

# Future Enhancements

Future capabilities may include:

- Barcode food scanning
- AI meal recognition from photos
- Personalized meal planning
- Grocery list generation
- Allergy and dietary restriction management
- Restaurant nutrition analysis
- Smart kitchen integration

---

# Expected Outcome

The Nutrition Management module empowers users to make healthier dietary choices through intelligent meal tracking, nutritional analysis, hydration monitoring, AI-powered coaching, and long-term wellness insights while maintaining privacy, flexibility, and complete user control.
