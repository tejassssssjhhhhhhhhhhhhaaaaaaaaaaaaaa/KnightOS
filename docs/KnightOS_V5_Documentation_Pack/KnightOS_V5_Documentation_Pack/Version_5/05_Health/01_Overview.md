# Knight OS Version 5 - Health Module Overview

# Overview

The Health Module is Knight OS's centralized wellness platform, designed to help users monitor, understand, and improve their physical and mental well-being. It consolidates health information from connected devices, user inputs, and supported health services into a single intelligent dashboard.

Rather than acting as a medical application, the Health Module serves as a personal wellness assistant by tracking habits, identifying trends, providing AI-powered insights, and encouraging healthier daily routines.

---

# Objectives

- Centralize health information
- Promote healthier habits
- Track wellness trends
- Support fitness goals
- Improve sleep quality
- Encourage proper nutrition
- Deliver proactive health reminders
- Integrate with connected health devices

---

# Core Components

The Health Module consists of:

- Fitness Tracking
- Sleep Monitoring
- Nutrition Management
- Health Reminders
- AI Health Insights
- Progress Reports
- Device Integration

Each component works together to provide a complete wellness experience.

---

# Health Dashboard

The Health Dashboard provides a quick overview of:

- Daily activity
- Steps taken
- Calories burned
- Active minutes
- Sleep summary
- Water intake
- Nutrition progress
- Workout completion
- Health reminders

The dashboard should update dynamically throughout the day.

---

# AI Integration

The AI assists users by:

- Identifying wellness trends
- Suggesting healthier routines
- Recommending workout schedules
- Monitoring sleep consistency
- Encouraging hydration
- Detecting inactivity
- Providing personalized wellness insights

Recommendations should remain supportive rather than intrusive.

---

# Device Integration

The Health Module may integrate with:

- Smartwatches
- Fitness bands
- Smart scales
- Heart rate monitors
- Sleep trackers
- Smartphones
- Health platform APIs

Data synchronization depends on user permissions and supported devices.

---

# Privacy

Health information is highly sensitive.

The module must:

- Require explicit permission
- Encrypt stored health data
- Allow complete data deletion
- Support export of personal health records
- Provide transparency regarding data usage

Users always remain in full control of their health information.
