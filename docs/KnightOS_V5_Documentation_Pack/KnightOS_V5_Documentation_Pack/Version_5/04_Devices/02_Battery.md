# Knight OS Version 5 - Battery Management

# Overview

The Battery Management module monitors, analyzes, and optimizes battery usage across all supported devices connected to Knight OS. It provides real-time battery insights, health monitoring, charging intelligence, and AI-powered recommendations to maximize battery life while maintaining optimal device performance.

This module works across smartphones, tablets, laptops, wearables, and supported IoT devices.

---

# Objectives

- Monitor battery status in real time
- Improve battery lifespan
- Reduce unnecessary power consumption
- Detect battery-related issues
- Provide intelligent charging recommendations
- Support multi-device battery monitoring

---

# Supported Devices

Battery monitoring should support:

- Smartphones
- Tablets
- Smartwatches
- Laptops
- Wireless earbuds
- Bluetooth accessories
- Smart home devices (where supported)

---

# Battery Information

For each supported device, display:

- Battery percentage
- Charging status
- Battery health
- Estimated remaining time
- Charging speed
- Power source
- Battery temperature (if available)
- Last charged time
- Last full charge
- Battery cycle count (if supported)

---

# Battery States

Possible battery states include:

- Fully Charged
- Charging
- Fast Charging
- Slow Charging
- Discharging
- Battery Saver Active
- Low Battery
- Critical Battery
- Overheating
- Battery Health Warning

---

# Battery Monitoring

The module continuously monitors:

- Current battery level
- Charge/discharge rate
- Background battery usage
- Screen-on consumption
- Application power usage
- Charging behavior
- Battery health trends

Updates should occur automatically with minimal battery impact.

---

# AI Battery Intelligence

The AI may provide suggestions such as:

- Enable Battery Saver
- Reduce screen brightness
- Close battery-intensive applications
- Delay non-essential synchronizations
- Optimize charging schedule
- Replace aging battery (when applicable)

Recommendations should always include a clear explanation.

---

# Battery Alerts

Users may receive alerts for:

- Low battery
- Critical battery
- Battery overheating
- Abnormal battery drain
- Charging completed
- Slow charging detected
- Battery health deterioration

Alert thresholds should be configurable.

---

# Battery History

Maintain historical information including:

- Daily battery usage
- Weekly trends
- Monthly trends
- Charging sessions
- Average screen-on time
- Battery health progression

Historical data helps identify long-term battery performance.

---

# Automation Integration

Battery events may trigger automations such as:

- Enable Battery Saver below 20%
- Disable background sync
- Reduce notification frequency
- Turn off Wi-Fi when idle
- Notify when fully charged
- Start charging reminder

All automations remain user-configurable.

- Maintain secure communication with connected devices
