# KnightOS User Guide

## Welcome to KnightOS
KnightOS is your proactive personal companion. It doesn't just store data; it understands your life.

## Core Features

### The Knight Orb
The Orb is your main interaction point. 
- **Tap**: Open the command center.
- **Glow**: Indicates Knight is thinking or has a suggestion.

### Knowledge Vault
Explore everything Knight knows about you. Data is categorized into 11 "Books" of knowledge.

### Life Atlas
A dynamic timeline of your past, present, and predicted future events.

### Security
Your data stays on your device. Knight uses hardware-backed encryption to protect your identity.

## Common Tasks
- **Connecting Accounts**: Go to Settings > Connectors to link Gmail, Health, etc.
- **Verifying Info**: Look for the "Verification Bubbles" in your feed to help Knight learn faster.
